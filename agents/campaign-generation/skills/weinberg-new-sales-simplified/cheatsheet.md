# Cheatsheet — New Sales. Simplified.

## New Sales Driver decision tree

- **Are new logos missing?** → Diagnose A / B / C (often more than one).
  - **A Targets**: No printed list, or list abandoned after one miss, or milk-run / CRM soup → build **finite, focused, written, workable** lookalikes (Ch 5).
  - **B Weapons**: Cannot tell a client-issue story, won’t/can’t call, organic first meetings → Power Statement, phone skeleton, 8-phase call (Ch 7–11).
  - **C Attack**: No blocks, inbox owns the morning, all time on Hot deals → time-block, math, ⅓-⅓-⅓, written plan (Ch 14).
- **Is it even a sales problem?** If strategy, pricing, or comp fights hunting, or talent < B-, fix that first (Ch 3–4).
- **Want a magic close?** Refuse. Run the basics inspection (list, effort, story, phone, call structure, time split, pipeline, plan) (Ch 16).

## How to pick targets

1. Answer Who/Why (best clients, why they buy, competitors, losses, near-misses, referrers).
2. Clone lookalikes. Path of least resistance; then focus like mad.
3. Print names for a defined period. Point at one page, not a pile.
4. Size to **workable** (enough to work thoroughly, not to surf).
5. Segment existing: Largest / Most Growable / Most At-Risk / Other → intentional imbalance.
6. If influencers *are* the sale, they get their own four-test list.
7. Add **4–5 dream** names with mini-plans; do not become a Prisoner of Hope.
8. First call **higher** in the org with issue language; walk down if referred. Never climb over a low-level no.

**Tells you’re targeting wrong:** folders-as-list; new vertical every month; equal time to every stop; one whale as the whole forecast.

## Sales-story components (sequence is the rule)

1. **Headline** — category in 1–2 sentences.
2. **Transition** — “[Buyer/role] turn to us when…”
3. **Client issues (3–7)** — pains, problems, opportunities, results. Spear tip. Emotional verbs.
4. **Offerings** — what you bill for. Short. Sandwich.
5. **Differentiators (≥5)** — why you win *those* issues. Premium price needs this.

**So What? test:** if they could say “we already have a ___,” you led with offerings. **No Issues = No Sale.** Internal source doc—excerpt, don’t hand out.

**Phone mini:** 2 issues (one sharp, one broad) ± 1 differentiator, then stop.

## When to pick up the phone

- **Default:** during a DND time block, against the printed strategic list. Inbound/social is a supplement, never a replacement.
- **Outside objective:** face-to-face visit. Do **not** overqualify lookalikes mid-call.
- **Inside objective:** full sales conversation (now or scheduled).
- **Open:** “Let me steal a minute” → “I head up…” → mini-statement → stop.
- **Never open:** “Did I catch you at a good time?” / “How are *you* today?”
- **If slammed:** let them go; call two is easier.
- **Ask three times** with **visit / fit / value**. First no is programmed.
- **VM (~3/4):** short snippet, “I’ll call again,” campaign, human on ~#3, never angry.
- **Tells you’re faking it:** news, LinkedIn, inbox, then lunch inside the block.

## Call structure (pilot-in-command)

| # | Phase | Do / don’t |
|---|--------|------------|
| 1 | Rapport + style | Their length; adapt speed/detail; no marlin small-talk |
| 2 | **Agenda** (highest leverage) | Time-check; preview flow; “what do *you* want?” |
| 3 | Clean up (existing only) | Junk before your news |
| 4 | Power Statement ≤3 min | Watch tells; don’t sell yet |
| 5 | Discovery | Personal / strategic / issues / process; two ears, one mouth |
| 6 | Sell | Their words; discovery already done |
| 7 | Fit + objections | “Might be a fit… what do you think?” |
| 8 | Next step | Mutual + **on the calendar** or the call failed |

**First meeting:** pad and pen, no projector. Sit 90°/beside. Never a presentation (Ch 13). If forced to pitch: title → agenda → they-turn-to-us-when → *their* situation → stop and talk.

## Attack math & time

- Blocks: **90–180 min**, on calendar first, hold them, stay outbound.
- Pipeline time: **⅓ Targeted / ⅓ Active / ⅓ Hot**.
- Healthy pipeline: full, moving, balanced.
- Reverse-engineer conversations from the close goal.
- Pre-book travel before meetings exist (commitment device).
