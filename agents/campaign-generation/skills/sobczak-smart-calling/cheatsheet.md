# Cheatsheet — Smart Calling Decisions

## Call-flow tree

1. **Intel first?** If you cannot state what they do, a current initiative/trigger, and a Possible Value Proposition (PVP) for *this* title → do not dial. CRM → their site → LinkedIn/news → social engineering (identify, help, because, questions).
2. **Objective?** Primary = what they will *do* this call (demo, meeting, comparison, order). Secondary = your guaranteed attempt if they will not (vendor name, future door, planted tripwire + where to file you).
3. **Live assistant?** Treat as buyer. Results, not products. Collect names, preferences, direct number. Do not “get past.”
4. **Voicemail vs keep hunting a human?** Always leave the **same** opening-as-message + “I’ll call [day]” + number twice. Opt-out to a person (0/#/* / wait) when you still need intel. Do not hang up at the beep.
5. **Buyer answers → opening (10 seconds)** You + company → one intel fact → PVP hint → weasel into questions. Then **stop presenting**.
6. **RRR (“not interested / all set / happy”)?** Soften + pattern-interrupt question. Never argue the reflex. Incumbent: better-than-satisfied, next eval, second source, stay-in-touch education. Fail → secondary.
7. **Questions** Benefit→circumstance→assumptive scene. Iceberg under the first answer. Capture their words. No “did you know we…” No “what do you like best about your supplier?”
8. **Receptive?** Transition → paraphrase *their* words → results only + trial closes → **ask** (Will you?), don’t wish.
9. **Continue later?** Why / you do / they do. They pick the date. Specific phone appointment. ACTION notes + summary email. No homework = no callback.

## Never say in the first 10 seconds

| Kill | Why |
|------|-----|
| “Just” calling / just wondering | Sounds weak |
| I’m introducing myself / we sell [product] with no result | Easy “we have that” |
| Take 10 minutes / I’ll be in your area | You take; they get nothing |
| Are you the person who…? | Announces a hunt |
| Can you help me? *(to the buyer)* | Their job is not to help you |
| If I could show you a way… you’d want that? | Commitment before content |
| Tuesday or Wednesday? / let’s meet | Decision before interest |
| Thanks for taking my call / sorry to interrupt | Self-minimizing |
| You’re a hard guy to reach / as you know we’re #1 | Pest or smarm |
| Do you have a few minutes? *(before PVP)* | Easy no |

Time, if at all: **after** PVP, as “if I’ve reached you at a good time…”

## Voicemail vs screener

| Situation | Do |
|-----------|-----|
| Box answers | Leave PVP opening; *you* call back; number twice; listen to full greeting |
| Live screener, buyer out | Social-engineer; get name, timing, direct line; optional written echo of the voicemail (even on their chair) |
| Screener asks “what’s this about?” | Instant results justification (cousin of the opening), then questions they can answer |
| Literature demanded | Qualify; if real, address *screener*, ugly/highlighted, Post-it, confirmed call time |
| Repeat touches | Same PVP imprint, different clock times |
| Pool exhausted | Last-resort: if I don’t hear back I’ll assume you don’t want to discuss it |
| Mystery name+number / fake cutoff | Never |

## Fast tells

- **Winging the opening** → you submitted a rough draft.
- **“I’ll send stuff and circle back”** → you scheduled amnesia.
- **Silence after price** → pause; do not discount into the void.
- **They named a person** → write it; “who is that?”
- **Scrolling past the big account** → absurdity dump, then dial with intel.
- **Email chime during the block** → you are not calling.
