## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**Fanatical Prospecting** is relentless interrupting to keep a qualified pipe full — anywhere, anytime, every channel. Superstars are not magically better closers; they prospect when times are good, bad, and when they do not feel like it. Empty pipe is the #1 sales failure; root cause is failure to prospect. You control only **actions, reactions, mindset**. There is no Easy Button. End the day with **one more call**.

**Interrupt, don’t debate “cold.”** Any unscheduled outreach to someone not expecting you is an interrupt (phone, door, email, text, social). People fear making the call, not the temperature of the list. Inbound rarely fills the pipe or reaches the largest accounts — interrupt those. Then **balance** the mix (phone, in-person, email, social, text, referrals, networking, inbound, shows) by industry, complexity, tenure, geo, and goal gap. “I’m better at…” is usually avoidance. Do not copy a 20-year veteran’s harvest mix if you are still building the database.

**Three laws of the pipe.** **Universal Law of Need**: the more you need the deal, the less likely you get it — desperation leaks and attracts failure. **30-Day Rule**: this month’s prospecting pays the next 90 days (transactional: One-Week Rule). Miss a month, wake up in a slump that feels like a closing problem. **Law of Replacement**: at 10% close, winning 1 statistically retires ~10 names — replace 10, not 1, or ride the roller coaster. 99% of slumps are prospecting failures. First rule: start prospecting; ~30 days of daily activity. Consistency creates “Sales Gods” luck; heroic desperate days do not.

**Know numbers: E + E = P.** Efficiency = activity inside the block. Effectiveness = outcome ratio and prospect quality (avoid LPPs). Track dials, contacts, emails, appointments, sales, social, texts, database adds — manually and visibly. You cannot be delusional and successful. Elite athletes know their stats.

**Kill the Three Ps.** Procrastination (you cannot eat the month in one day). Perfectionism (research in Platinum Hours, not as a Golden Hour shield; messy success > perfect mediocrity). Paralysis from analysis (what-ifs). True job-hate is a career choice; typical “call reluctance” is 3Ps. Disrupt: one call, then the next.

**Time: Golden vs Platinum + Power Hours.** Same 24 hours. **Golden Hours** = prospect, qualify, present, close. **Platinum Hours** = lists, research, CRM after the block, proposals, social, write-and-schedule email. CEO of You, Inc.: say no, prioritize (fired for missing number, not admin), delegate, know $/Golden Hour. **Horstman’s Corollary**: work contracts to the time allowed — 30 focused minutes beat an unfocused day. **Power Hours**: 1 hour, one activity, DND, no dings, 25–50 tele-dials; three hours can yield ~120. Do not open email first (Iannarino). Do not time the market; **eat the frog** first thing. Multitasking is a myth; 7 dials/hour becomes 47 when power is concentrated.

**Four Objectives — pick one per touch.** (1) Dual-calendar **appointment** (not “call me maybe”). (2) **Gather/qualify** — don’t swing at ugly; define the strike zone. (3) **Close** on transactional live channels. (4) **Build familiarity** (usually secondary; primary via SPC on cold conquests). Touch counts: inactive 1–3; familiar + in window 1–5; cold unknown **20–50**.

**Prospecting Pyramid + CRM.** Do not treat the database as a square. Tip = in-window and hot inbound/referrals (call first, while fresh). Then conquests. Then fill holes and find windows. Bottom = unknown/wrong data — gather. List quality (after mindset) drives the block. Own the CRM like a CEO: every interaction in, no pocket of cards, celebrate qualifying nuggets. Trash-can CRM = wrong person, wrong time, wrong message.

**Law of Familiarity.** Familiarity lubricates the yes to time. Five levers: daily prospecting; referrals (experience + **ask**; customer/personal/professional); networking (listen, not pitch; 24-hour follow-up); brand; personal brand (**speak in public**). Familiarity Threshold (first-name text/social inbox) only on high-value names. Warning: familiarity work is easy hiding; balance with today’s interrupts.

**Social is not selling.** Most important tech since the phone for context and BIC mapping — not a panacea. Phone/email conversion dwarf social. Five objectives + **Five Cs** (connect, create, curate, convert, consistency). 30–60 min Platinum blocks. LinkedIn first for B2B; 2–4 channels. Profiles are the spear. Weave social into outbound (triple threat, triggers); 6–24 months of gravity will not hit this quarter’s number.

**Message: WIIFM + because + ask.** Seconds to earn time. Prospects meet for their reasons. 10-second good-enough **because** (Langer) beats a novel VP; C-level gets a **strategic bridge**, volume lists a **targeted bridge**. Emotion first, then insight, then logic. Assumptive ask ~70% vs ~30% passive. **Shut up** (rule of thirds: yes / real no / maybe). Manufacture confidence (posture, smile in voice). Congruence or they won’t trust you.

**Phone is the #1 tool.** Contact often 15–80% (business services ~25–40%); “nobody answers” is usually “nobody dials.” Five steps, no pauses, no “how are you.” VM when it matters: number four times, 30 seconds. Outside reps: one hour of dials covers more than a day of doors; then spend in-person on hubs.

**RBOs: Anchor. Disrupt. Ask.** Rejection is personal to the brain — manage it, don’t deny it. Reflex (auto script), brush-off (“send info”), objection (truth + because). Do not **overcome**. Unexpected agree/takeaway. Never “I understand.” Two RBOs then leave. **NEXT.** Persistence on conquests: GKs get **please ×2**, transparency, no tricks; sidestep early/late, InMail, handwritten; hacks = other extensions, fellow salespeople, around back (no security).

**Email: delivered, opened, convert.** 10–20× social response. One-to-one; no bulk/images/links/attachments/spam words. Subject 3–6 words, no question, them-centered. **AMMO** then Hook–Relate–Bridge–Ask with a specific time. Triple threat with VM + social. Proof before send. Same craft on social inboxes.

**Text only after familiarity.** Standalone ~4.8% convert; after phone **+112.6%**. Post-event, trigger resource, nurture value, congratulations. ID yourself, <250 chars, full URLs, one retry then other channels. Never cold-text. Never text and drive.

**IPP: hub-and-spoke, not windshield tourism.** Least efficient channel. Plan 3–5 spokes around phone-set appointments; **T-Call** the T; sales goggles; close-kit in the car; listen, don’t pitch; log same day.

**Grit.** Mental toughness > talent. Four pillars: written **desire**; **outlearn = outearn** (15 min/day ≈ 21 books/year); body (30–60 min move, 7–9h sleep, **eat breakfast**); attitude (expect to win; everything happens for a reason; gratitude). After a win, **attack yourself** — 30-Day Rule does not pause for trophies. Mantra: *When it is time to go home, make one more call.* Last question: **How bad do you want it?**
