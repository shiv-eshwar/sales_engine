# Chapter 13: Social Selling

## Core Idea
Social is the most important sales technology since the telephone — context, not just contacts — but **social selling is not selling** and not a panacea. Phone and email conversion dwarf social. Use social to enhance, elevate, and sometimes accelerate balanced outbound, never as the exclusive channel. Contact/conversion on phone and email beat social; hanging out waiting to be interrupted sells nothing.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Five Objectives of Social Prospecting (outcomes)**: (1) Personal branding and familiarity, (2) inbound via education/insights, (3) trigger-event and buying-cycle awareness, (4) research/information gathering, (5) outbound via direct engagement. Also: SPCs. Time must grow pipe size and viability or it is TV with a login.

- **Five Cs of Social Prospecting (effectiveness)**:
  1. **Connecting** — everything starts with a connection. Send LinkedIn after every new meeting (highest accept probability; anchors familiarity; unlocks their network for BIC mapping). Personalized note. Three creation modes: **direct** (LI/FB request), **reciprocal** (follow on Twitter/G+ so they follow back), **passive** (they connect because your content adds value — strongest).
  2. **Content creation** — original articles, video, slides, podcasts, infographics, white papers, cases, books. Positions you as expert; likes/shares reveal new names and buying emotion. Hard; marketing may block it.
  3. **Content curation** — feed the beast daily without original volume. Three pillars: **awareness** (industry, thought leaders), **intent** (strategic clusters + your takeaways, not random shares), **tools** (aggregate/automate). Never lead with product plugs — banned, blocked, spam.
  4. **Conversion** — plan for leads and conversations; social as a small inbound machine with intent.
  5. **Consistency** — grind, not hit-or-miss. Block **30–60 minutes/day in Platinum Hours**; cumulative impact is the point.

- **Five tool categories (efficiency)**: curation (Feedly, Pocket, news), creation (Pulse, YouTube, SlideShare, Canva), distribution (Hootsuite, Buffer — load off-hours), engagement/analytics, intelligence (Google Alerts, native social intel). Expect shrinking free tiers; companies own the data and will charge.

- **Channel choice**: Go where the money (prospects) is. Manage **2–4 channels** (better at 2). LinkedIn is default for B2B/high-end B2C. Comfort matters for consistency; if they live on a channel you hate, learn to like it.

- **Social + outbound = mashed potatoes and gravy**: Social is long-term, passive, nuance; 6 months–2 years to pull gravity from a small audience. Combined: amplify familiarity, better lists, trigger timing, nurture ahead of windows, contact data, **BIC (buyer-influencer-coach) mapping**, qualify, relevant outbound messages. HubSpot and LinkedIn themselves combine inbound + outbound.

- **SPC on social**: Decision-maker/influencer mapping, nurture, coaches, familiarity, Law of Reciprocity (value first), get invited in or reduce friction on the first ask. Limit to a handful of conquests.

## Key Concepts
- **Social selling ≠ pitching** on LI/Twitter/FB; people want connect, interact, learn. Social inbox can supplement email (see Ch 19).
- **Activity set**: research, networking, lead gen, inbound, prospecting, trigger monitor, competitive intel, social CRM, account management.
- **Profile as spear tip**: professional same headshot (genuine smile → competence/likeability/influence), cover image, first-person conversational summary (who, values, what you do best, why trusted), contact info (privacy is not a sales strategy), media/links, custom URL, complete history, quarterly refresh. “Would you buy you?”
- **First impressions are unfixable online**. Subtle killers: poor/incomplete profiles, no/unprofessional photo, opinionated politics/religion, TMI.
- **Think before you post**: familiarity is a two-edged sword; negative impression builds walls. Always onstage.
- **Inbound via insight**: share/publish problem-solving content; embed gated assets inside articles rather than spammy “download now” posts.
- **Trigger events**: status-quo disruptions; follow without connecting; lists/circles; job-changers who already bought from you.

## Mental Models
- Use social for **familiarity, research, nuance, chess with stakeholders** — not for this week’s quota.
- Use **where they hang out** as the only channel filter that matters.
- Use **Platinum social blocks** when Golden Hours are being eaten by the feed’s dings, likes, and badges.
- Prefer **interrupt + social weave** over **social-only Buyer 2.0 mythology**.

## Anti-patterns
- **Social as panacea / telephone is dead**: gurus who cold-email or cold-call you to sell “never cold call.”
- **Connection-count theater**: accepts, likes, group posts, zero revenue.
- **Pitching in the feed**: reputation damage.
- **All channels, no depth**: exhaustion and dilution.
- **Golden Hour scroll**: social designed to hook you; same as parking in front of TV.
- **Waiting 6–24 months for gravity** while number is due this quarter.

## Worked Example
New rep declares the phone dead after a LinkedIn webinar (“Buyer 2.0,” “old school”). Challenge: one week, his social-only vs. Blount opening the database and interrupting.

Day 1: rep — 7 connection accepts, “good prospects,” sold $0 (“it takes time”). Blount — 73 dials, 19 contacts, 2 closed with cards. Week: Blount **17 new paid accounts**; also used social (VM + email + LI ping drove 2 callbacks; scanned profiles for call relevance; connected after conversations). Rep: connections, comments, follows, content — **sold nothing**. After a $10 bet and commission math, rep agrees: interrupt plus weave social into a balanced mix.

Profile fix (same day): one professional smiling headshot everywhere, complete LI summary in first person, phone/email on the profile, quarterly review.

Daily: 30–45 Platinum minutes — connect everyone met yesterday with a personalized note, curate 3 intentional shares, scan alerts for triggers on conquests, then shut it down.

## Key Takeaways
1. Social is mandatory in the mix and the best familiarity/research channel — it is not selling and not a replacement for outbound.
2. Phone/email conversion crush social; social-only weeks produce connections, not cash.
3. Five objectives + Five Cs + time-boxed consistency; tools buy efficiency, not a pipe.
4. Profiles are the spear; first impressions do not get a second chance.
5. Connect after every meeting; curate with intent; create if you can; convert on purpose.
6. Pair social with interrupts (BIC mapping, triggers, relevant because).
7. Go where prospects are; LinkedIn first for B2B; 2–4 channels max.
