# Chapter 22: Eleven Words That Changed My Life

## Core Idea
This chapter is thin by design: one mantra, not a new framework. **When it is time to go home, make one more call.** Fanatical prospectors do not enjoy prospecting more than losers do. They pay for success in advance with the extra mile (Gary Ryan Blair) — one more call, sometimes two, three, or four.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Eleven-word mantra**: When it is time to go home, make one more call.
  - When to use: End of the day, end of the week, weather, burnout, “good” justifications to knock off early, after a string of losses.
  - How: Index card over the desk (or equivalent). Last thing you see before you hit the street or hang up. Do the extra dial anyway.
  - Why it works / failure mode: Many “one more” touches become sales — serendipity that looks like the Sales Gods (Ch 5) is often just the extra attempt others skip. Skipping it is how average days stay average.

## Key Concepts
- **Extra mile**: Distance between consistent achievers and people who merely follow.
- **They get tired too**: Hunger, wavering resolve, hate of the activity — same as failing reps. Difference is discipline, not liking it.
- **Universe-rewards-sticking** (practitioner reading): not mysticism — the last call is still a statistical at-bat you would have forfeited.

## Mental Models
- Use the **eleven words** as a hard stop-rule: the day is not over at the planned last dial.
- Think of extra calls as **prepaid success**, not heroics.

## Anti-patterns
- **Knocking off when the calendar says you “did enough.”**
- **Waiting to love prospecting** before doing the extra one.
- **Confusing exhaustion with a completed standard.**

## Worked Example
*(Thin chapter — one artifact, not a padded case.)* Index card taped above the desk: “When it is time to go home, make one more call.” Friday, dark, pipeline ugly. Justification: weather, fatigue, “I’ll crush it Monday.” Make the extra call. Prospect buys on the spot — the Friday-afternoon message Blount says he gets from trainees constantly. Income that does not exist if the card is ignored.

## Key Takeaways
1. The extra call is the whole chapter.
2. Top performers hate the work too; they do it anyway.
3. Success is paid in advance with hard things, including one more interrupt.
