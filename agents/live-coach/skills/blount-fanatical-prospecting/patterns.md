# Patterns — Fanatical Prospecting

## Balanced Channel Mix
**When to use**: Designing the week; “I’m better at one channel”; copying a veteran or a guru.
**How**: Weight phone, in-person, email, social, text, referrals, networking, inbound, shows by industry, complexity, tenure, geo, and goal gap. New territory = more interrupt-and-qualify. Tenured = more window-timed calls, referrals, nurture. High-density urban may favor in-person; rural favors phone/email.
**Trade-offs**: The disliked channel (usually phone) is often the highest leverage. Balance is slower to feel “natural” than hiding in a favorite medium.

## Pyramid-First List
**When to use**: Every Power Hour.
**How**: Sort CRM by qualify × potential × probability. Morning: in-window + inbound/referrals. Then conquest nurture. Then information-gathering on the lower mass. One objective + one channel per list.
**Trade-offs**: Building lists is Platinum work; skipping it feels faster and destroys effectiveness.

## Golden / Platinum Split
**When to use**: Calendar design.
**How**: Golden Hours = live selling interrupts and meetings. Platinum = lists, research, CRM after the block, proposals, social, write-and-schedule email. First 1–2 hours = eat-the-frog phone.
**Trade-offs**: Family/life pressure; max income requires some platinum on edges of the day.

## Power Hour (Horstman)
**When to use**: Missed dial standards; all-day “prospecting” that yields 2 calls/hour.
**How**: 30–60 min, DND, devices/email off, pre-built list, notes on paper, CRM after. Goal: 25–50 tele-dials/hour; three hours can yield ~120. Chunk 10-call bites.
**Trade-offs**: Intense and rejection-dense; do not mix research/logging/social into the hour.

## Five-Step Phone Interrupt
**When to use**: Any unscheduled live call (cold, warm, inbound, customer).
**How**: Name → you + company → reason (objective) → because (targeted or strategic) → assumptive ask → shut up. Never “How are you?” Never pause before the ask.
**Trade-offs**: Feels abrupt; that is the point. RBOs still come — you just get to them fast.

## Voicemail When It Matters
**When to use**: Conquest or in-window names. Skip on unqualified spray lists (VM tax 20–30s; single-digit callbacks).
**How**: ID; number twice; reason; curiosity because; name + number twice; ≤30 seconds.
**Trade-offs**: Burns block time; only pay the tax when callback or familiarity is worth it.

## Anchor–Disrupt–Ask (RBO)
**When to use**: Reflex, brush-off, or objection after the ask.
**How**: Ledge (“that’s why I called” / “fantastic”); unexpected agree/takeaway/specify; re-ask a time. Two RBOs then leave. Never “I understand.” Never argue them wrong. “Send info” → “specifically what?”
**Trade-offs**: Scripts need rehearsal or they sound canned; fighting feels righteous and loses.

## Dual-Calendar Appointment
**When to use**: Any “yes” to meet.
**How**: Specific day/time/place on both calendars + send the invite. Reject “anytime mornings.”
**Trade-offs**: You’ll log fewer “appointments”; show rates and sanity rise.

## Strike Zone then Window
**When to use**: Complex, contractual, budgeted, multi-threaded deals.
**How**: Profile the ideal buyer; information-gather until window and stakeholders are known; nurture locked-qualified; appoint when the window opens. Transactional/noncontractual: appoint first, qualify in the meeting.
**Trade-offs**: Over-qualifying can be call avoidance; under-qualifying fills pipe with ugly.

## Familiarity Campaign (SPC)
**When to use**: Cold high-value list (20–50 touches if unknown).
**How**: Layer VM, email, social, events, referrals, speaking. Five levers. Limit full Familiarity Threshold investment to conquests.
**Trade-offs**: Easy to hide in branding and empty the pipe (Ch 12 warning).

## Triple Threat Open
**When to use**: Email must get opened by a hard name.
**How**: VM + social ping/comment + AMMO email (any order). Short 3–6 word subject, no question, them-centered. Hook–Relate–Bridge–Ask with a specific time.
**Trade-offs**: Over-layering without a plan becomes annoyance.

## Hub-and-Spoke IPP
**When to use**: Outside, local, SMB, walk-in possible.
**How**: Phone-set hubs; 3–5 nearby spokes; T-Call the T; close-kit in the car; CRM same day. Do not IPP-only.
**Trade-offs**: Least efficient channel; random driving burns Golden Hours.

## Gatekeeper Professional + Hacks
**When to use**: Walled DMs.
**How**: Likeable, please×2, transparent, relationship, no tricks, ask for help, sidestep (early/late, social inbox, handwritten, events). Hacks: other extensions; press 1 for sales; around back if no security.
**Trade-offs**: Tricks blacklist you; persistence still required on conquests.

## Text Only After Familiarity
**When to use**: Post-event, trigger, nurture, congratulations — not cold.
**How**: ID + company, <250 chars, full URLs, one retry then other channels. After phone, conversion can jump ~113%.
**Trade-offs**: Third-rail channel; strangers experience it as domain violation.

## Replacement Math
**When to use**: After every win/loss; after a “full pipe, now we only close” month.
**How**: Replace at close-ratio scale (10% close → replace ~10 per win). Daily prospecting never pauses for closing season.
**Trade-offs**: Feels like you should “just close”; that is the roller coaster.

## Slump Protocol
**When to use**: Nothing closing; recycling stale names; hunting new closes.
**How**: Audit prospecting 90 days ago (30-Day Rule). Stop calling dead names. Daily blocks ~30 days. Own the blame. NEXT after ugly calls.
**Trade-offs**: Results lag activity; desperation wants instant miracles.

## One-Call / One-More Discipline
**When to use**: 3Ps; end of day; analysis paralysis.
**How**: One dial, then the next. When it is time to go home, make one more call. Eat the frog at dawn.
**Trade-offs**: Never becomes fun; that is not the criterion.
