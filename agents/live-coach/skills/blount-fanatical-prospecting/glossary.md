# Glossary — Fanatical Prospecting (Jeb Blount)

**30-Day Rule** — Prospecting in this 30-day window funds the next 90 days (short-cycle: One-Week Rule). Miss a month, slump in 90. (Ch 5, 21)

**AMMO** — Email plan: Audience, Method, Message, Outcome. (Ch 19)

**Anchor / Disrupt / Ask** — Three-step RBO turnaround: ledge for the brain, unexpected pull, assumptive re-ask. Not “overcome.” (Ch 16)

**Assumptive ask** — Ask as if yes is coming. Assertive ~70% yes vs ~30% passive. (Ch 14–15)

**Balanced Prospecting Methodology** — Mix phone, in-person, email, social, text, referrals, networking, inbound, trade shows; weights from industry, product, tenure, geo, goal gap. No one true way. (Ch 4)

**Because / bridge** — Reason they give time (WIIFM). Targeted (class of buyers) vs strategic (one conquest/C-level). Langer: “because” itself lifts compliance. (Ch 14–15, 19)

**BIC mapping** — Buyer–influencer–coach map, often via social. (Ch 13)

**Brush-off** — Polite conflict-avoidance no (“send information,” “call me later”). (Ch 16)

**Buying window** — Period they can actually buy (contract, budget, trigger). Qualify it before burning appointments on complex deals. (Ch 9–10)

**CEO of You, Inc.** — Own time, territory, CRM, results; scarcest resource is time. (Ch 8, 11)

**Concentrate your power** — One activity per block; no multitasking. (Ch 8)

**Conquest prospects** — Limited list of best/largest accounts (10–100) for nurture, mapping, triggers, familiarity. (Ch 10, 12)

**Dual-calendar appointment** — Real meeting: on both calendars, specific time/place. “Call me maybe” is not an appointment. (Ch 9)

**Eat the frog** — Do the hardest, highest-leverage task first (morning phone block). (Ch 15)

**Effectiveness** — Outcome ratio and quality of activity (vs LPPs). (Ch 6)

**Efficiency** — Activity generated inside the allotted block. E + E = P. (Ch 6, 8)

**Fanatical Prospecting** — Extreme, uncritical enthusiasm for keeping a qualified pipe full via relentless interrupting across channels. (Ch 1)

**Familiarity Threshold** — First-name, low-intrusion communication (incl. text/social inbox) even when not buying; invest only on high-value names. (Ch 12, 20)

**Five Cs of social** — Connecting, Content creation, Curation, Conversion, Consistency. (Ch 13)

**Five Levers of Familiarity** — Daily prospecting; referrals/intros; networking; company/brand; personal brand (speak in public). (Ch 12)

**Five-step phone framework** — Name → identify → why calling → because → ask + shut up. No “how are you,” no pauses. (Ch 15)

**Four Objectives** — Set appointment; gather/qualify; close sale; build familiarity. (Ch 9)

**Golden Hours** — Prime selling time: prospect, qualify, present, close. Not admin. (Ch 8)

**Horstman’s Corollary** — Work contracts to fit the time allowed (inverse of Parkinson). (Ch 8)

**Hub-and-spoke / T-Call / IPP** — Plan in-person calls around appointment hubs; look right/left/behind for extra doors. (Ch 18)

**Interrupt** — Unscheduled outreach to someone not expecting you and not in an active sales discussion. The real unit of prospecting; “cold vs warm” is semantics. (Ch 3)

**Law of Familiarity** — More they know you/brand/company, more they engage. Cold may take 20–50 touches; inactive/familiar 1–10. (Ch 9, 12)

**Law of Replacement** — Close 1 at 10% close rate → statistically replace ~10, not 1. Ignore it → roller coaster. (Ch 5)

**Law of Triviality** — Waste time on low-value work while mission-critical selling waits; often 50%+ leakage. (Ch 8)

**LPPs** — Low Potential Prospects; activity that looks busy and starves the pipe. (Ch 6, 10)

**NEXT** — After a vicious rejection, dismount the dead horse and dial the next name. (Ch 16)

**One more call** — “When it is time to go home, make one more call.” (Ch 1, 22)

**Platinum Hours** — Before/after Golden: lists, research, CRM, proposals, social, scheduled email. (Ch 8)

**Please, please** — Gatekeeper connect technique: please twice. (Ch 17)

**Power Hours** — Focused 30–60 min teleprospecting blocks (Sales Gravy: three hours → ~120 dials). (Ch 8, 15)

**Prospecting Pyramid** — Database as ranked layers from unknown mass to in-window tip; start mornings at the tip. (Ch 10)

**RBOs** — Reflex responses (auto scripts), brush-offs, objections (truth + because). (Ch 16)

**Rule of thirds** — After you ask and shut up: ~1/3 yes, ~1/3 real no, ~1/3 maybe/RBO. (Ch 14)

**Sales Gods** — Apparent luck from cumulative daily prospecting. (Ch 5)

**Seven Mindsets** — Optimistic, competitive, confident, relentless, thirsty for knowledge, systematic/efficient, adaptive (Adopt–Adapt–Adept). (Ch 2)

**SPC (Strategic Prospecting Campaign)** — Multi-channel long campaign, often familiarity-first on conquests. (Ch 9, 13)

**Strike zone** — Ideal-customer profile + when to engage before the window; don’t swing at ugly. (Ch 9)

**Three As** — Adopt new practices, adapt them, become adept. (Ch 2)

**Three Ps** — Procrastination, Perfectionism, Paralysis from analysis. (Ch 7)

**Three Things You Can Control** — Actions, reactions, mindset. (Ch 1, 21)

**Triple threat** — Layer VM + social ping + email to raise familiarity/opens. (Ch 19)

**Universal Law of Need** — The more you need it, the less likely you get it; desperation from an empty pipe. (Ch 5)

**WIIFM** — What’s in it for me? — their reason to give time. (Ch 14)
